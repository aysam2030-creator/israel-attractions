## 2026-07-12 - Memoizing react-leaflet properties to prevent DOM thrashing
**Learning:** When working with react-leaflet in this codebase, ensure Leaflet properties (like `L.divIcon`, polyline paths, and options objects) are cached or memoized to maintain stable object references across renders and prevent severe performance bottlenecks from DOM thrashing. An effective way to cache dynamically generated objects that depend on variable arguments inside render loops is to use an external `Map` to store and retrieve instances by a stringified key, avoiding hook violations and preventing DOM thrashing.
**Action:** When adding or modifying Leaflet components, ensure that properties passed to them (such as arrays for pathing, or objects like `L.divIcon`) maintain stable references across renders via caching or `useMemo`.
