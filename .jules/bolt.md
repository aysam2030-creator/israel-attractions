## 2026-07-19 - Cached Leaflet objects prevent thrashing\n**Learning:** In react-leaflet, passing inline objects to props like `icon` (via `L.divIcon`) or `pathOptions` causes severe DOM thrashing, as Leaflet destroys and recreates DOM nodes on every render due to referential inequality.\n**Action:** When working with react-leaflet, always cache or memoize Leaflet properties (like `L.divIcon`, polyline paths, and options objects) using external Maps, `useMemo`, or constants outside the component to maintain stable object references.
